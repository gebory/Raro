RARO
ARCHIVE OF MARKS
An evolving archive of symbols, stories and marks inspired by memory, place and meaning.
WHY RARO EXISTS
Modern fashion moves quickly. Ideas disappear before they are understood. Stories fade before they are told. RARO was created as an archive where meaning comes before aesthetics, and where every mark exists for a reason.
THE MARK
The RARO Mark is inspired by an ancient Mesopotamian rosette. Refined into a contemporary symbol, it represents the archive itself. Not a collection. Not a product. The archive.
